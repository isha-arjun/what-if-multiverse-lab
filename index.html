Project notes

A playground for alternate realities

WHAT IF? — Multiverse Lab is an unofficial fan-made creative experiment. It uses original summaries and a lightweight rules-based engine to remix public-facing character and event names into hypothetical branches. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Marvel or Disney. It does not represent official canon.

How the engine works

Pick a character, event and decision. The simulator applies weighted effects to stability, threat, alliance cohesion and technology, then generates a causal chain from those values.

Why thousands?

Combinations of character focus, event, location and choice create a large scenario space. The count is calculated from the current library; outcomes are generated from reusable rules rather than thousands of copied scripts.

Names and references belong to their respective rights holders. All scenario text and simulation logic here are original fan-made material.
